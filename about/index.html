YOUR COACH

Hi, I’m Eoin.

I’m a runner and coach based in Toronto. I started Performance Running Toronto to work with runners who want to improve and would like some help figuring out how.

I’ve represented Ireland twice in cross country and run a 2:26 marathon. Running is a big part of my life, and I enjoy helping other people get more out of theirs, whether that means finishing a first race or chasing a time they’ve had in mind for years.

As your coach, I’ll explain what each session is for, check in on how you’re getting on and adjust the plan when needed. Work, family and tired legs all affect training. We’ll take those into account rather than trying to force every week to look perfect.
