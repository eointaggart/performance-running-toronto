BUILD ON YOUR AUTUMN

Take a breather.
Then keep building.

After an autumn race, a break is often exactly what you need. Once you’re ready, we’ll find a training rhythm you can keep through the colder months.

Winter training doesn’t need to mean chasing race fitness every week. We’ll build consistency, develop your endurance and bring in more specific work as your spring race gets closer.

I’m opening 10 new places so I can keep giving each runner the time and attention their training needs.

THE TRAINING

A winter with direction.

01 / FIND YOUR RHYTHM

Start where you are

We’ll look at your recent training, recovery from autumn races and the time you have. Your starting point will reflect how you’re feeling now.

02 / BUILD CONSISTENCY

Make the weeks count

Easy running, long runs and controlled sessions, with the balance and volume suited to you. We’ll adapt around holidays, dark evenings and winter conditions.

03 / PREPARE TO RACE

Get specific

As your race approaches, training will become more specific to its distance and demands. We’ll work through pacing, fueling and how to approach race day.

The timing of each stage depends on your experience, current training and target race.

BEFORE YOU START

A few practical
questions.

Do I need to have a race booked?

No. Tell me what you’re considering and we can work out a suitable goal. You can also start by building a consistent routine.

What happens when the weather is bad?

We’ll adjust the session to suit the conditions. That might mean moving a workout, using a treadmill or changing the effort. The plan should help you train consistently through winter.

Can I do this outside Toronto?

Yes. Individual coaching is available online. The High Park group session is there for runners who can join in Toronto.

When would I start?

Send me an enquiry with your spring goal and recent training. We’ll discuss a suitable starting point and timing, including any recovery you need after an autumn race.
